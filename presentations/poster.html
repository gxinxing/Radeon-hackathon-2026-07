5 Critical Bug Fixes
  • 1
    Floating base lock
    URDF world_joint commented out + merge_fixed_links=True → robot ignores gravity. Fixed: uncomment + merge_fixed_links=False
  • 2
    Termination unit mismatch
    base_euler in degrees, term_pitch in radians (0.52° vs 30°) → 1-step termination. Fixed: use degree values directly
  • 3
    Obs history timing
    _build_low_level_obs updated history before physics step → temporal misalignment. Fixed: read obs_buf only, update after step
  • 4
    Conservative local optimum
    approach_ball=1 → reward +34 but ball distance unchanged (standing still). Fixed: curriculum + approach_ball=10
  • 5
    Action std explosion
    entropy_coef=0.01 → std 5.78, noise dominates policy. Fixed: entropy_coef=0.003 → std 0.07
