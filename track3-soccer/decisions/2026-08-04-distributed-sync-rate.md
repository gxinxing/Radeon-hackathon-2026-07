## Decision: Rate-limit distributed 3v3 coordination to a configurable 2 Hz
## Context: The coordinator produced two TCP frames at 50 Hz while six independent Genesis workers consumed only about 2–3 updates per second. Ordered socket backlogs delayed the terminal `MSG_END` frame, so a physically completed match was correctly rejected as incomplete.
## Alternatives considered: Keep 50 Hz and extend shutdown grace; add a full request/ack or latest-state transport; run the distributed launcher at a configurable rate matched to worker throughput.
## Reasoning: A 2 Hz launcher default prevents unbounded FIFO backlog, preserves independent robot control and role assignment, and is the smallest change aligned with the competition scope guard, which explicitly does not require a new real-time communication architecture. The rate remains configurable for faster hardware and the coordinator's legacy direct default remains compatible.
## Trade-offs accepted: World-state coordination is lower frequency than physics and high-level control. This is acceptable for the evidence/demo path, but a future real-time system should replace rate matching with explicit backpressure or acknowledged latest-state delivery.
